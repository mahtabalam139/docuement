Most Expected 2026 Interview Scenario

Scenario: Trading Application Down at Market Open (9:15 AM)

What interviewer expects
Raise P1 Incident
Join bridge call
Check application logs
Check Linux server
Verify database
Verify network connectivity
Check market feed
Communicate to stakeholders
Restore service
Perform RCA
Sample Answer

During market opening, traders reported orders were not getting executed. I immediately declared a P1 incident, initiated a bridge call with Infrastructure, Network, DBA, and Application teams. Linux health checks showed the application was healthy, but database connections were exhausted. We temporarily increased the connection pool limit and restarted affected services. Trading resumed within 15 minutes. RCA identified a slow-running query introduced in the previous release. A permanent query optimization and monitoring alert were implemented to prevent recurrence.
