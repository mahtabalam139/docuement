SECTION 12: DEADLOCK QUESTIONS
Q32. What is Deadlock?

Two transactions waiting for each other.

Example

Transaction A locks table A.

Transaction B locks table B.

Each waits for other lock.

Manager Round Special Question
Q57. What is your approach to Database Troubleshooting in Production?
Ideal Answer

"My troubleshooting approach is structured. First, I identify whether the issue is connectivity, performance, locking, blocking, storage or query-related. I use tools like SHOW PROCESSLIST, pg_stat_activity, EXPLAIN ANALYZE, application logs and infrastructure metrics to isolate the root cause. My priority is always service restoration, followed by RCA and preventive improvements such as indexing, query optimization, monitoring and capacity planning. In trading environments, I focus heavily on minimizing downtime and ensuring that order flow, risk validation and market data processing remain unaffected."
